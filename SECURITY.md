# Security Policy

## Supported state

Quantara is in a foundation and specification phase and has no released executable version. Security support applies to the latest commit on the default `main` branch. Historical commits, forks, provider systems, third-party datasets, and unapproved local modifications are not supported versions.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Do **not** open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting form:

[Open a private security advisory](https://github.com/wyze69-sys/Quantara/security/advisories/new).

Include, when available:

- affected file, commit, or future version;
- impact and realistic attack scenario;
- reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- required permissions and environment;
- suggested remediation or relevant references;
- whether the issue has been disclosed elsewhere.

Do not include real credentials, private provider data, account secrets, or harmful production payloads. Use minimal synthetic evidence.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith and coordinate disclosure based on impact, reproducibility, and remediation readiness. No fixed response or remediation timeline is promised during the foundation stage.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please allow reasonable time for investigation before public disclosure. Do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it, access data that is not yours, degrade services, or use social engineering.

## What belongs elsewhere

- A market-data integrity or provenance failure without a security impact belongs in the **Data-quality incident** issue form.
- A normal defect belongs in the **Bug report** form.
- Harassment or community-conduct concerns must be reported through the private contact in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), not through a security advisory.

When in doubt and public disclosure could increase risk, use the private vulnerability-reporting form.
